Etali's Compact

A charter for playing strong Magic together

The goal is to play and enjoy the strongest decks allowed by our restrictions. If a game is unpleasant, the restrictions are incrementally updated — never frozen.

Current Restrictions

  • No Game Changers
  • No 2 Card Infinites
  • No Free Interaction If a player is tapped out, they cannot interact.
  • Budget Cap — $500
  • No Games After Supper
